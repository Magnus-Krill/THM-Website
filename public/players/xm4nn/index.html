Background Oct 2024 – Mar 2026

There from the first week, and again at the top.

xm4nn_, also known as yung tickle monster, was one of the first members of the clan when mingailas founded it in October 2024, and a co-founder of Sonnenrad alongside him.

He was co-owner by the time mingailas left on 4 August 2025, and took the clan over. THM finished paving the cardinal highways to the world border under him, on 2 March 2026. He retired for personal reasons on 21 March 2026 and promoted Ryazor before leaving.
