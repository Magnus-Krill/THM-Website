Background Mar 2025 – present

Took over the bot, kept the axes moving.

MagnusKrill joined THM in March 2025 and was leading inside the year. He won the first THM highway tournament and has been a fixture on the +X and +Z axes since.

He picked up Kitbot when Ooranges retired in January 2026, and wrote the THM Meteor Client add-on from scratch a few days later. Most of the clan’s automated highway work runs through it. He was also on several of the core team’s sky logo griefing runs.
