Background Nov 2024 – present

Co-owner in a month, owner in sixteen.

Ryazor first joined 6b6t on 12 February 2024 and THM that November. He was made co-owner in December 2024 and held that seat through both of the clan’s handovers, until xm4nn_ promoted him to owner on 21 March 2026.

The clan’s biggest non-highway build happened under him: the largest sky logo on 6b6t, 33 million blocks as of 3 June 2026. Before THM he was one of the founding members of the Air Destroyers.
