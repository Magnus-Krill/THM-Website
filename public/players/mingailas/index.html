Background Oct 2024 – Aug 2025

Started it as Moon.

mingailas founded the clan on 19 October 2024 under the name Moon, with CapyKing10, CaT_345, Volizray and xm4nn_. It was renamed The Highway Men within the week, once the crews had settled into the Nether. He was also one of the leaders of Sonnenrad.

He ran THM through its founding era and the 100,000-blocks-a-day stretch that made it the successor to the Highway Extension Group. On 4 August 2025 he stepped down as owner following serious allegations and left the community; xm4nn_, then co-owner, took over.
