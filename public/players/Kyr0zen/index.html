Background Jun 2025 – present

Three days on the server, then in the clan.

Kyr0Zen did freelance art before 6b6t and had been taking commissions since January 2020. He joined the server on 2 June 2025, applied to THM after two days of playing, and was accepted on the 5th. He worked up to co-owner by 27 January 2026.

He won the 6b6t Halloween logo competition in October 2025 and the Christmas one that December. On 30 May 2026 he became assistant manager of the 6b6t marketing team, where he runs the 6b6t Hollywood Discord that feeds the official YouTube channel.

“What goes on under the highways is none of our business.”
